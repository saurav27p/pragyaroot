Questions

Answers, plainly.

Four things students ask most often. If your question is not here, write to us.

Is PragyaRoot really free?
Yes. Every note, solution, and drill. No accounts. No paywalls. No ads. There is no paid tier in the works. This is a study resource, not a business.
Can I print chapters and use them offline?
Yes, and this is deliberate. Every chapter has a print stylesheet that turns the page into a clean worksheet — the accent colour is chosen so it survives a black and white photocopier at a Nepali copy shop, and margins are set so it staples neatly into an A4 booklet. PragyaRoot also installs as an app and works offline for pages you have already visited.
Is this aligned with the CDC syllabus?
Yes. Every chapter is checked against the Curriculum Development Centre syllabus for Class 10 in Nepal, and rechecked whenever the syllabus changes. The date of the last check is visible on each subject page.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. PragyaRoot is built mobile first. It works on any phone with a modern browser, supports light and dark mode, and once loaded works offline for the pages you have already visited.
